Privacy Policy

Sheikh Zahid Quran Academy collects information submitted through its Free Trial and contact forms, such as a student's name, email address, WhatsApp number, country, age group, course preference and availability. We use this information to respond to enquiries, arrange lessons and provide academy services.

If you submit a review, your first name, selected course and review text may be displayed publicly. Do not include private contact or sensitive information in a review.
