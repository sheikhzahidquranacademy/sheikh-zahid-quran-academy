HELPFUL INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions

Are classes one-to-one?

Yes. Lessons are arranged individually for each student.

Can I choose a male or female teacher?

Yes. Students or parents can request their preferred teacher gender.

Do you offer Pakistan-based and UK-based teachers?

Yes. Both options are available, with separate package pricing.

Are class recordings provided?

No. Class recordings are not provided. Monthly progress reports are shared.

Can lesson times suit my country?

We aim to arrange lesson times around the student's local time zone and availability.
