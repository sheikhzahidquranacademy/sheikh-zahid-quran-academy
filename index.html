WELCOME TO OUR ACADEMY

About the Academy

Sheikh Zahid Quran Academy is an international online Quran academy dedicated to authentic, high-quality Islamic education for children and adults. Students can learn through personalised one-to-one classes with male and female teachers based in Pakistan or genuinely resident in the UK. Our teaching supports learners across the United States, Australia, New Zealand and Europe.

✧

Our Mission

To help learners build a lasting connection with the Quran through accessible teaching, steady practice, understanding and Islamic values.

FAQs · Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer a free trial class?

Yes, one free trial class is available for new students.

For which age groups are classes suitable?

We offer learning options for children, teenagers and adults.

Are classes one-to-one or in groups?

Classes are private, one-to-one online lessons.

Can I select my preferred class time?

Yes. Timings are arranged around availability and the student's local time zone.

Can I choose a male or female teacher?

Yes, you may request a male or female teacher, subject to availability.

Is the Quran taught with proper Tajweed?

Yes. The Tajweed course covers rules, articulation points (makharij) and recitation improvement.

Are class recordings provided?

No. Class recordings are not provided.

Which languages are available?

Lessons and communication are available in English, Urdu and Arabic.

Can children and adults both enrol?

Yes. Our courses are available for children, teenagers and adults.

Do you provide progress updates?

Yes. Students receive monthly progress reports.

Do you have teachers from Pakistan and the UK?

Yes. Pakistan-based and UK-based teachers are available as separate package options.

How can I get started?

Complete the free trial form or contact us directly on WhatsApp.
