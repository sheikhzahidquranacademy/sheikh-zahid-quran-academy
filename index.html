WELCOME TO OUR ACADEMY

Quran learning that fits your life.

Sheikh Zahid Quran Academy is an online learning space for students at every stage—from taking the first steps with Noorani Qaida to developing fluent recitation, memorisation and deeper understanding. Every student learns through individual classes, with lesson timing arranged around their local time zone.

Courses for every stage.
